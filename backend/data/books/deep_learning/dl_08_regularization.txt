TITLE: Generalization and Regularization
SOURCE: Deep Learning (Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville) - https://www.deeplearningbook.org/
---
Generalization is performance on unseen data, not just training batches. Overfitting appears when training loss drops but validation loss rises. Regularization techniques reduce overfitting by constraining model behavior.

Dropout randomly disables activations during training to prevent co-adaptation. Weight decay penalizes large parameter magnitudes. Batch normalization stabilizes intermediate distributions and can accelerate optimization. Early stopping halts training when validation metrics stop improving.

Regularization choices depend on dataset size and noise. Small datasets usually need stronger regularization and augmentation. Evaluation should include confidence calibration and error analysis by subgroup, not only aggregate accuracy.
