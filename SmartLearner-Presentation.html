Problem & Solution
SmartLearner

The Problem

  • Traditional content does not adapt to individual learner differences.
  • It is hard to know what each student completed and when intervention is needed.
  • Weak connection between assessment, learning path, and continuous improvement.

Our Solution

  • Adaptive placement test at the start of the journey.
  • AI-generated personalized syllabus based on strengths and weaknesses.
  • Analytics Agent delivers continuous actionable recommendations.
